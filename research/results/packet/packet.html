Office plan review

Each page shows two plans for the same brief. You are reviewing concept layouts only: no code, structural or fire compliance is claimed. Rooms are drawn as rectangles; circulation is the uncoloured floor.

  • Function: Do the rooms work for what the brief says the company does? 1 = unworkable, 5 = I would use it as drawn.
  • Space utilisation: Is floor used well: sensible room sizes, no dead space, no cramped rooms? 1 = wasteful or cramped, 5 = efficient.
  • Circulation: Can people reach every room clearly and comfortably from the entrance? 1 = confusing or blocked, 5 = obvious and comfortable.
  • Daylight: Do the rooms that need daylight get it? 1 = poor, 5 = well placed.
  • Furniture usability: Could you fit the furniture these rooms need, with clearances? 1 = no, 5 = comfortably.
